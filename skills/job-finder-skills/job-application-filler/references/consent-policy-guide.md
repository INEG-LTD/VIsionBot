# Consent Policy Guide

Use the stored policy values from CUSTOM RULES.

## Policy Values

- `auto_allow`: accept the field automatically
- `auto_deny`: decline the field automatically
- `ask_user`: ask the user before choosing

## Policy Categories

- `marketing_opt_in_policy`
- `sms_consent_policy`
- `talent_pool_policy`

## Always Auto-Accept

- Required terms and conditions
- Privacy acknowledgement
- Accuracy certification

## Always Follow Policy

- Marketing emails
- Promotional outreach
- SMS updates
- Future opportunities or talent community enrollment

## When To Ask Instead Of Guessing

- The field is required and its category is unclear
- The consent text mixes multiple purposes in one checkbox
- The form combines legal consent with marketing or recruiting outreach in a way you cannot separate
